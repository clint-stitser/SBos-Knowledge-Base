Layer 01 — The System · Personal Tools

Today, Horizon & Me

Your personal side of S-BOS — the working list, the daily operating sheet, and your life domains. Everything runs on the same tasks the rest of the system uses.

Home → The System → Personal Tools
The Big Idea
One task, everywhere

The personal tools live in the “Me” group in the sidebar. They are not a separate app or a second to-do list — they are a personal lens on the exact same records the whole system runs on.

The rule that makes this work: a follow-up is always a task. When you flag a note for follow-up, when an audit finds a deficiency, when a GYR report calls for action — that becomes a real task, attached to whatever it came from, carrying a breadcrumb back to its source. Nothing lives in a separate “follow-ups” pile that has to be scanned. If it needs doing, it’s a task, and it shows up on your Horizon.

Because of that, the three tools below never invent data. They read your tasks and show them three different ways: the full list (Horizon), today’s focus (Today), and your goals and identity (Me).

The Tools
Three views of the same work
Horizon
Your working list
Every task assigned to you, grouped into rings by urgency — Overdue, This week, Active, Coming soon, Parked. Triage each one: Done (with optional proof of completion), Kill (not applicable), Hide (tuck it away), or Update (add a note). Tap the star to add a task to today’s focus; tap the pin to make it the Main. This is where you decide what matters.
Today
The daily operating sheet
A read-out of the focus you set on Horizon: Top 1 — the Main (your anchor), Secondary 2, and the Hit List. Your live Google Calendar runs down the left with color-coded time blocks. A day-mode pill (Focus / Buffer / Free) sets the posture for the day. Focus on the few; relax and play the game.
Me
Your life domains
Four personal departments — Body, Being, Balance, Business — each with its own goals, vision, and principles. Personal goals live on your family trust as the parent entity, so goals you share (kids’ running miles, family fitness) reach everyone in it, while your own stay yours.
On Every Task
Proof, notes & provenance

Open any task and you get the full task-management surface, wherever it appears:

  • Proof of completion — when you mark a task Done, attach a photo or file as evidence, or complete it without proof. The file is stored against the task like any other S-BOS attachment.
  • Notes & comments — a running thread on the task, which also rolls into the project’s consolidated Notes timeline.
  • Breadcrumb — every task shows where it came from (“Q2 2026 Loan Reconciliation › Checklist › Loan #010”, “Note › …”, “Report › …”), so you always know the context.
Getting Things In
Capture — type it, say it, or snap it

You rarely create tasks by hand. Open Kompass (the sparkle button, on every page) and just say what’s on your mind — it splits a brain-dump into the right items and files each one. Kompass captures:

  • Tasks & reminders, notes, decisions, journals, meeting notes, and a food/meal log — routed to the right home automatically.
  • Photos. Drop or snap a photo and Kompass reads it, then splits it into items — a whiteboard of to-dos becomes several tasks, a page of meeting notes becomes meeting notes plus action items, a plate becomes a food log. Big phone photos are shrunk automatically so they upload cleanly.

It always matches before it creates — if something already exists, it updates that record instead of making a duplicate.

Daily Rhythm
How to use it
  1. Morning — set the day on Horizon. Scan your rings, star your three, pin the Main.
  2. Work from Today. Top 1, then Secondary 2, then the Hit List, with your calendar alongside. Set your day mode.
  3. Capture as you go. Anything new — type it or snap it into Kompass; it lands in the right place and shows back up on Horizon.
  4. Check Me weekly. Are your Body / Being / Balance / Business goals moving?
