What is S-BOS?

S-BOS — the Stitser Business Operating System — is the integrated technology platform that connects people, processes, data, and AI across all Stitser BUILT entities. It is not a single app. It is an orchestrated stack of purpose-built tools, each playing a defined role, wired together so information flows without friction and every team member can do their best work.

The most important design principle: Softr is the team-facing interface. Christine, Andi, Mary, and every team member access S-BOS through the Softr portal. They never need a SmartSuite seat. SmartSuite is the database underneath — powerful, structured, always up to date — but the team sees it through the clean, task-focused views that Softr provides.

Claude agents connect to S-BOS through Kompass, the MCP server that gives Claude read/write access to SmartSuite and Google APIs. When a team member invokes a Claude skill — say, "Baseline this project" — Claude is not guessing. It is reading live data, running a defined workflow, and writing structured records back into the system.
