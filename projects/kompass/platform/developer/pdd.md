*Stub — to be built out. See `platform-pdd.md` for shared architecture.*

## Target User

Principals running land development, LIHTC, infill, or value-add projects.

## Unique Brain Structure

- Entities and legal structures
- Deal type taxonomy (SFR subdivision, LIHTC multifamily, infill, flip, land hold)
- Capital stack components (equity, construction debt, permanent debt, gap financing)
- Entitlement phase tracking (pre-app → tentative map → final map → permits)
- Investor and partner profiles (7-section model adapted for JV relationships)

## Domain-Specific Decision Matrices

- Deal go/no-go (returns threshold, market conditions, partner requirements, risk tolerance)
- Partner selection (track record, capital commitment, control provisions)
- Lender selection (rate, recourse, construction draw process, relationship)
- Exit criteria (hold vs. sell vs. refi by asset class and market conditions)

## Domain-Specific Skills

- Underwriting Model
- Pay App Skill
- Baseline Capture
- Entitlement Tracker
- LIHTC / Capital Stack Analyzer
- Compliance Audit (brokerage disclosures)

## Domain-Specific Routines

- Monthly Pay App Reminder
- Entitlement Deadline Monitor
- Market Conditions Brief (weekly)

## Knowledge Library Domains

- SFR Land Development (phases: acquisition → entitlement → civil → construction → close-out)
- LIHTC / Affordable Housing (4% and 9% credit mechanics, capital stack, compliance)
- Multifamily Finance (cap rates, debt structures, waterfall models, HUD programs)
- Entitlement and Planning Law (NRS, tentative maps, parcel maps, variances, CUPs)
- Construction Finance (pay apps, lien law, NRS Chapter 108, bond requirements)
- 1031 Exchange and DST (QI, identification rules, DST structures)
