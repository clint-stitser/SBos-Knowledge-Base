# Personal Kompass — Product Design Document

*For Clint, Personal Kompass is The Stitser Way. Full PDD at: `projects/s-bos/build-docs/TSW_Product_Design_Doc.md`*

## Target User

Individual operators who want a personal operating system — goals, relationships, health, finances, decisions — with an LLM that knows the whole person.

## Unique Brain Structure

- Areas of life (the taxonomy Clint uses for the Morning Ritual)
- Relationship profiles (7-section model adapted for personal relationships — family, friends, mentors)
- Health data integration (Oura → weight, sleep, readiness, activity)
- Financial position (summary level — not transaction-level accounting)
- Personal decision log (life decisions: career moves, investments, relationships, commitments)

## Domain-Specific Skills

- Morning Ritual
- Evening Ritual
- Health Tracker
- Relationship Coach
- Weekly Review
- Journal (WAR stack, Cash stack, Gratitude stack, etc.)

## Domain-Specific Routines

- Daily Horizon Scan (personal areas of life)
- Weekly Review trigger (Friday)
- Health trend summary (weekly)
