# Personal Kompass

**Who:** Individual operators who want a personal operating system independent of their business role

**Job:** Manage personal goals, relationships, finances, health, and decision history with an LLM that knows the person, not just the task.

**Note:** For Clint, Personal Kompass IS The Stitser Way. See `projects/s-bos/build-docs/TSW_Product_Design_Doc.md` for the full TSW PDD.

## Brain Categories

- Personal goals and commitments (areas of life)
- Relationship profiles (family, close friends, key relationships)
- Financial position (net worth, cash flow, investment summary)
- Health and fitness (weight, sleep, readiness, activity)
- Personal decision log
- Rituals and routines (Morning Ritual, Evening Ritual, Weekly Review)
