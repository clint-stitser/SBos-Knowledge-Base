# Contractor Kompass

**Who:** General contractors and construction managers running project-based businesses

**Job:** Manage job cost, subcontractor relationships, pay app lifecycle, and schedule risk across multiple projects simultaneously.

## Brain Categories

- Projects (job cost by phase and trade)
- Subcontractor performance (ratings by job)
- Change order history
- Pay app status by project
- Lien waiver tracking
- Knowledge library (construction phases, NRS lien law, bond requirements, job cost accounting)
