# Contractor Kompass — Product Design Document

*Stub — to be built out. See `platform-pdd.md` for shared architecture.*

## Target User

General contractors and construction managers running project-based businesses.

## Unique Brain Structure

- Jobs (not projects — contractor terminology)
- Cost codes and phase structure (CSI or custom)
- Subcontractor database with performance ratings
- Change order log linked to each job
- Pay period and lien waiver tracking

## Domain-Specific Decision Matrices

- Bid go/no-go (margin threshold, client history, capacity)
- Subcontractor selection (license, bond, insurance, track record)
- Change order approval (scope, pricing, schedule impact)
- Hire criteria (role-specific, capacity-based)

## Domain-Specific Skills

- Job Cost Tracker
- Subcontractor Scorecard
- Change Order Logger
- Lien Waiver Manager
- Schedule Risk Flagging
- Pay App Skill (adapted for GC role)

## Domain-Specific Routines

- Weekly Job Cost Flash
- Subcontractor Invoice Follow-up
- Schedule Milestone Alert

## Knowledge Library Domains

- Construction phases and sequencing
- NRS Chapter 108 — Nevada lien law
- Bonding and insurance requirements by project type
- Change order mechanics and dispute resolution
- Job cost accounting (WIP schedules, overbilling/underbilling)
- Subcontractor agreement fundamentals
