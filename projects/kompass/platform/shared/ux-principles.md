# User Experience Principles

These principles govern how every Kompass product should feel to use.

## 1. Conversation first, configuration never
The operator talks. The assistant figures out what to do. No forms, no wizards, no onboarding flows that feel like homework. The first five minutes should produce a tangible result.

## 2. The Brain grows invisibly
Context accumulates without the operator noticing. They do not click "save" after a conversation. They do not tag entries. Facts are extracted and filed automatically. The only signal that the Brain is growing is that responses get sharper over time.

## 3. Draft, don't decide
The assistant drafts. The operator decides. Every external action waits for operator approval. The Feed is the approval queue. Nothing ships without a tap.

## 4. Surface the right context at the right time
The operator should not have to remember to ask for prior decisions, vendor history, or decision criteria. The assistant surfaces these automatically based on context.

## 5. The system gets smarter the longer you use it
The Decision Log is the most powerful compounding asset. Every decision recorded — with rationale and outcome — becomes a reference point for the next similar decision.

## 6. Mobile and voice first for capture; desktop for review and analysis
Operators are not at desks. Quick Capture, voice notes, and share sheet integration are the primary capture mechanisms. Review, analysis, and planning sessions happen on desktop.
