# Start Here: your first tour of S-BOS

*S-BOS Foundations · Onboarding walkthrough — read this first, then the five
foundation articles it links to.*

---

Welcome to Stitser Built. You've just joined a company that decided the
software running the business should be built the way we build everything
else: from a blueprint, with the right tool for the job, and with nothing on
the truck that the work doesn't call for. S-BOS isn't an off-the-shelf system
we squeezed ourselves into — it's our operating system, shaped around how
projects actually move from dirt to done, and it gets sharper every week
because the people using it help build it.

That last part is not a slogan. The system ships with an AI teammate —
Kompass — whose job is to make sure you never start from a blank table and
never wait in line for "the software person." Describe what you need, and the
system assembles it with you. This walkthrough shows you the four things you
need on day one: where you live, what a record page is, how to ask Kompass
for things, and where knowledge lives.

## 1. Where you live: your department surface

Everything in S-BOS hangs on a grid — vertical departments (product lines
like Entry-Level Housing or Retail) that carry projects dirt → done, and
horizontal departments (Estimating, Finance, Accounting…) that apply one
skill across all of them, with decision gates G0–GX as the spine. Read
[The Grid](the-grid.md) for the full picture; the original whiteboard sketch
is preserved in
[department-review-illustration.pdf](department-review-illustration.pdf).

Your home screen depends on which kind of department you're in:

- **In a vertical?** You get a **dashboard**: your projects laid across key
  situations — current gate, green/yellow/red health, next key date — with
  your department's time-bound goals on top.
- **In a project-driven horizontal** (like Estimating)? Your screen IS your
  tool, with a project dropdown scoped to your gate window and a pipeline of
  what you owe, to which project, by when.
- **In a routine-driven horizontal** (like Accounting)? You get a
  **scorecard** that renews each period.

Details and examples: [Surfaces](surfaces.md).

## 2. What a record page is: launch pad + toolbelt

Open any project, company, person, or loan and you land on its **details
page** — the launch pad. Across the top are tabs: that record's **toolbelt**.

The rule is the craftsman's truck: every tool in the company is always
available (the truck), but a record only carries what its work calls for (the
toolbelt). Your department's blueprint sets the defaults — a brokerage deal
gets a simple offers-and-closing-statement budget, a subdivision master
project carries the underwriting module — and the **Tools** button on the
record page lets you adjust the belt for one record.

Full story: [The Tool Library](tool-library.md). And if you're curious why
the whole system is shaped as tasks → checklists/budgets/schedules →
projects → priorities → goals, that's
[the bottom-up ontology](task-ontology.md).

## 3. How to ask Kompass to do things

Kompass is the AI layer wired into S-BOS. It reads and writes live records,
and its defining job is captured in
[Kompass builds tools with you](kompass-tool-builder.md): observe your
workflow, break it into blocks, match blocks against what already exists, and
assemble the result on the right record — seeded from templates so you never
start blank.

Try it on day one:

> "Set up a budget on <your project>."

Kompass activates the budget tool on that project and seeds it with the full
blueprint template (51 lines for land development) — you edit from a
populated foundation instead of building from scratch.

The habit to build: **when something feels manual, tell Kompass what you're
trying to do.** Either the tool already exists and it hands it to you, or
you've just written the spec for the next slice of the system.

## 4. Where knowledge lives

Remember the ontology: knowledge + tool + technique = skill. The tools live
in the platform; the knowledge and techniques live here, in the
**S-BOS Knowledge Base** — and in `kb_entries` inside the system itself.

- **This Foundations series** — the five articles linked above — is the
  mental model.
- **Workflows, agents, and roles** sections of this KB cover the specific
  procedures.
- **If you learn something the KB doesn't know, it goes in the KB.** A tool
  without a documented technique isn't a skill yet.

## Your first-week checklist

1. Read [The Grid](the-grid.md) — 5 minutes, explains every screen you'll see.
2. Open your department surface and find your projects (or your pipeline, or

3. Open one record, press the **Tools** button, and look at the toolbelt.
4. Ask Kompass to do one thing for you.
5. Skim the other foundation articles as they become relevant:
